Markets & economy

  1. Payrolls tripled the forecast; unemployment held at 4.1%

    August added 162,000 jobs against a 55,000 consensus, and the S&P 500 gave back 0.38% as the 10-year yield rose to 4.78%, per TheStreet.

  2. Rate-hike odds for September reached 63%

    Treasury yields jumped as futures priced a 63% chance the Fed hikes this month, with July's payroll reading revised from negative to positive, per Charles Schwab.

  3. Lululemon fell to seven-year lows on cut guidance

    The retailer cut its full-year outlook as China sales fell 4% year over year, one of Friday's sharpest single-stock moves, per Investrade's market review.

  4. Bitcoin holds above $81,000 on heavy ETF inflows

    Friday brought the largest spot-ETF inflow in nine months after Fed governor Waller's comments, per Yahoo Finance. The move leaves bitcoin up 28.1% over the past month, though still 27.3% below a year ago.

  5. The docket: PPI Thursday, CPI Friday, Fed next week

    Markets are closed Monday for Labor Day; then PPI lands September 10 and CPI September 11, the last inflation reads before the FOMC's September 15–16 meeting, with the funds rate at 3.50%–3.75%, per GO Markets.

Top headlines

  1. Germany's AfD poised for a state-election breakthrough

    A win in Saxony-Anhalt would give the far right its first state governorship since World War II, per NPR.

  2. Gloria Steinem dies at 92

    Tributes from politics and entertainment poured in for the pioneering feminist organizer and writer, per NPR.

  3. ICE officer charged with lying about a shooting

    The Justice Department says the officer misrepresented the circumstances of a Venezuelan immigrant's shooting, per NPR.

  4. Heat deaths far exceed official counts

    An NPR investigation with Boston University finds heat kills far more Americans than the records show.

  5. Scientists map protein interactions behind profound autism

    Research published in Science charts the interactions and points to new targets for drug development, per NPR.

Tech & AI

    Working largely on its own for 11 days, the model produced 13 million lines of Lean proving the theorem from Lean's three standard axioms, per Anthropic.

  2. Anthropic's IPO marketing moves to mid-October

  3. OpenAI says Astra is harder to monitor

    GPT-6's recurrent-depth design reasons in latent space rather than visible chains of thought, and OpenAI's own testing flags the lost oversight, per Implicator.

  4. Figure books $3.5 billion of GPU compute

    The Nscale partnership deploys up to 100,000 Nvidia GPUs to train Figure's Helix humanoid models, per the companies' announcement.

  5. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all stumbled this week

    All three suffered brief outages within two days of one another — even frontier labs take the occasional long lunch — per Crypto Briefing.
